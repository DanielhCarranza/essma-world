# Content, Localization, and Child Safety — Essma World

## Mexican-Spanish writing rules

- Ship all child-facing text in `es-MX`. Use familiar, warm words and short sentences.
- Lead with verbs and clear actions: “Vestir”, “Elegir”, “Listo”, “Volver”, “Sorpresa”.
- Pair important text with a recognizable icon; never rely on a symbol alone for a critical action.
- Avoid shame, threat, romance pressure, sarcasm, or competitive language. A player may stop or retry any activity safely.
- Record each string with an ID, context, character limit, and accessible label. Keep future adult-facing translations separate from child-facing copy.

### Voice examples

| Situation | Approved direction |

## Cultural care

Sonora is a living place, not a generic desert theme. Creative work should reflect local landscapes, wildlife, food, crafts, language, and celebrations with care.

- Identify the factual source or local collaborator for culture-specific content.
- Review place names, wildlife behavior, traditional garments, food, and celebrations before final art or copy.
- Do not treat sacred, ceremonial, Indigenous, or family traditions as costume props or collectible rewards without informed review and permission.
- Avoid caricatures, vague “Mexican” decoration, and mixed-region imagery presented as Sonoran fact.
- Credit collaborators in a parent-accessible credits surface when appropriate.

## Child safety requirements

- No ads, in-app purchases, gambling-like rewards, social feeds, user-to-user messaging, public profiles, location access, or behavioral analytics.
- No required login, email, phone number, camera, microphone, contacts, or social account.
- Do not show external links in child-facing screens. Any future external action is parent-gated and plainly explained.
- Keep save data on device in v1. Backup files are initiated only in a parent-only surface and contain no personal data by design.
- Collect no names, photos, voice recordings, IP-derived profiles, or identifiers beyond normal host operation. Reassess privacy obligations before any backend or analytics change.

## Parent gate

The backup/restore screen uses a simple adult gate that does not pretend to be strong identity verification: a readable caregiver prompt plus a brief hold/confirmation action. It prevents accidental child taps, not a determined adult or child. The design must state this limit and never ask the child for personal information.

## Accessibility baseline

- Large touch targets; visible keyboard focus; semantic buttons for React UI.
- Text alternatives for icons and important scene interactions.
- Captions/textual equivalents for meaningful spoken content and sound-only instructions.
- Independent toggles for music, sound effects, and reduced motion; preference applies before any scene starts.
- Clear contrast, color-independent status signals, no rapid flashing, and no time pressure in the initial release.

## Content review checklist

Before release, verify language, cultural source/review, age rating suitability, asset rights, readable Spanish labels, safety restrictions, and accessibility requirements for every new player-facing pack.
