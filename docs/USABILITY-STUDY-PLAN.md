# Essma World — Child Usability Study Protocol (P0.1)

This protocol guides moderated, caregiver-consented visual usability sessions with children aged 4–6.

## Non-negotiables & Privacy

- **Zero Data Collection**: No names, dates of birth, accounts, audio/video recordings, telemetry, or personal data.
- **Caregiver Consent**: A parent or legal guardian must be present and provide verbal/written consent before beginning.
- **Pressure-Free**: The child can pause, leave, or stop at any time. There are no test scores, timers, or wrong choices.

## Target Audience & Cohort

- 3 to 5 children in target age range (4 to 6 years old).
- Mixed pre-reading and early-reading experience.

## Session Procedure (10 Minutes)

1. **Introduction (1 minute)**:
   - Hand the device (tablet or laptop) to the child showing the Essma World home map screen.
   - Prompt: "¡Hola! Este es el mundo de Essma. Puedes tocar lo que quieras."

2. **Task 1: Discover Rancho (2 minutes)**:
   - Observe if the child independently taps the open Rancho node on the map.
   - Success metric: Enters ranch without verbal instruction.

3. **Task 2: Dress a Companion (3 minutes)**:
   - Observe if the child taps Essma or a companion character, chooses a wearable item, and sees the visual change.
   - Success metric: Successfully equips at least one clothing layer or accessory and returns to the ranch.

4. **Task 3: Patio Decorating & Undo (3 minutes)**:
   - Observe if the child taps "Decorar", places a patio decoration item, and tests moving or undoing a placement.
   - Success metric: Places at least one item on the patio and understands the visual feedback.

5. **Task 4: Save & Reopen (1 minute)**:
   - Refresh the page and ask the child: "¿Sigue estando tu rancho como lo dejaste?"
   - Success metric: Character appearance and patio decor are restored accurately.

## Observation Recording

Record only non-identifying behavioral notes:
- Task success (Independent / Assisted / Confused).
- Touch target readability on mobile (390×844) vs desktop.
- Any verbal comments or visual joy reactions ("¡Qué bonito!").
- Any points of friction or unclear icons.
